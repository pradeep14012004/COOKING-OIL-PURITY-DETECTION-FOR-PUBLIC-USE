Popular Oils Unveiled

Sunflower Oil

“The Everyday Fryer”

Quick Tip

High in Vitamin E — great for frying. Avoid repeated heating to limit harmful breakdown products.

Mustard Oil

“Bold & Flavorful”

Did you know?

Mustard oil adds a punch of flavor and has antimicrobial qualities — but use proper high smoke-point techniques for frying.

Coconut Oil

“The Tropical All-Rounder”

Heat-Stable

Coconut oil stays stable at high temperatures — great for frying and baking. Watch portions: it's high in saturated fats.

Olive Oil

“Heart-Healthy Choice”

Health Note

Rich in monounsaturated fats and antioxidants — perfect for salads and low-heat cooking, but avoid heavy frying.
